### 🚀 About Me

I am a results-driven **Data Analyst & Data Scientist** and Computer Engineering graduate with hands-on industry experience at **DeetsDigital** and **1Stop.ai**. I specialize in bridging the gap between raw, complex datasets and executive business decision-making through automated data pipelines, predictive machine learning models, and intuitive interactive dashboards.

- 🔭 **What I Do:** Build robust end-to-end data analytics pipelines, interactive Power BI & Excel dashboards, advanced SQL relational databases, and predictive machine learning models.
- 💼 **Industry Experience:**
  - **Data Science Intern @ DeetsDigital:** Engineered automated demand forecasting pipelines (safety stock optimization) and unsupervised PCA-DBSCAN anomaly detection models for 34,000+ drone flight telemetry records.
  - **Data Science Intern @ 1Stop.ai:** Built classification models (Random Forest, Decision Trees with SMOTE) processing 280,000+ credit card transactions for real-time fraud detection.
- 🎓 **Education:** B.E. in Computer Engineering, Gujarat Technological University (CGPA: 8.20 / 10).
- 💡 **Core Passion:** Crafting visually stunning Healthcare, FinTech, Environmental, and Retail BI dashboards, uncovering hidden patterns through ER diagrams and EDA, and translating analytical metrics into measurable business value.
